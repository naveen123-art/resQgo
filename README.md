# 🚗 ResQGo

### Smart Vehicle Emergency Assistance Application

ResQGo is a smart vehicle emergency assistance application designed to help users quickly find and connect with nearby mechanics and automobile workshops during vehicle emergencies.

The application provides emergency assistance features, location-based mechanic discovery, workshop services, service-provider management, notifications, billing, and payment functionality.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of ResQGo is to provide a convenient and reliable platform for vehicle owners to get assistance during unexpected vehicle breakdowns or emergencies.

Instead of searching manually for nearby mechanics or workshops, users can use ResQGo to find available service providers and request assistance based on their location.
